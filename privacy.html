Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

Introduction

Startly.run ("Startly," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address. We use passwordless authentication (magic links), so we never collect or store passwords.
  • Race Data: Race names, dates, distances, locations, goal times, finish times, and notes you enter into the app.
  • Checklist Data: Custom checklist items and templates you create.
  • Photos: Race photos you upload (Pro feature). Photos are stored securely and only accessible to your account.
  • Contact Information: If you contact us via the website or email, we collect the information you provide in your message.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version for compatibility and debugging purposes.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous usage patterns to improve the app experience. We do not track individual behavior or create user profiles for advertising.
  • Location Data: We do not collect GPS location. Race locations are entered manually by you and used solely to provide weather forecasts.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Create and manage your account
  • Generate race checklists and countdowns
  • Provide weather forecasts for your race locations
  • Process your subscription (if you upgrade to Pro)
  • Send you important service-related communications
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Period.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Open-Meteo: Weather data for race locations. We share only the race location (city/region) to fetch forecasts. Open-Meteo is a free, open-source weather API.
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management for Pro purchases. RevenueCat processes your payment through the App Store or Google Play—we never see your payment card details.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Cloud infrastructure for data storage and hosting. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored on secure servers with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
  • Authentication uses secure, time-limited JWT tokens with refresh token rotation.
  • We limit access to personal data to employees who need it to operate, develop, or improve the Service.
  • Local data on your device is stored using the platform's secure storage mechanisms.

Data Retention

  • Your data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • You can export all your data at any time from the app settings (JSON format).
  • You can delete your account at any time, which permanently removes all your data from our servers within 30 days.
  • We may retain anonymized, aggregated data for analytics purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Export all your data in JSON format from the app
  • Correct: Update your information at any time within the app
  • Delete: Delete your account and all associated data
  • Restrict: Contact us to restrict processing of your data
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app settings or contact us at privacy@startly.run.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal cookies for essential functionality only (e.g., keeping you logged in). We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics that track you across sites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@startly.run
  • Web: startly.run/contact
