Why I Built This

I've run everything from 5Ks to marathons, and the one problem no running app ever solved was the chaos of race morning. I'd train for months, only to show up at the start line wondering if I packed gels, whether my bib was pinned right, or if I'd remembered Body Glide.

Every running app wants to track your miles or plan your training. Nobody was helping with the part that actually keeps you up the night before a race: Am I forgetting something?

So I built Startly — the app I wish I'd had before my first marathon. It's simple, focused, and built for the real moments that matter: the night before and the morning of.
